# TahaTyper Pro: Google Docs & Scheduler Guide 🤖📄🕒

Welcome to the most advanced version of TahaTyper! This guide will show you how to use the new **Google Docs integration** and **Automated Scheduler** to type flawlessly, even when you're away from your computer.

## 1. Google Docs Integration 📝
TahaTyper Pro is designed to work seamlessly with Google Docs. Since Google Docs runs in your web browser, TahaTyper uses high-level simulation to type directly into your active document.

### How to use with Google Docs:
1.  **Open your Google Doc** in Chrome or your preferred browser.
2.  **Paste the text** you want to type into the "Content to Type" box in TahaTyper.
3.  **Click into your Google Doc** so the cursor is blinking where you want the typing to start.
4.  **Press F6 (Start)**. TahaTyper will give you **3 seconds** to make sure the Google Doc window is active before it starts typing naturally.

## 2. Automated Scheduler 🕒
The Scheduler allows you to set specific dates and times for TahaTyper to start typing automatically. This is perfect for completing tasks at a specific time (like 6 PM on April 23rd).

### How to set a schedule:
1.  **Enter your text** in the "Content to Type" box.
2.  **Set the time** in the Scheduler box using the format: `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` (e.g., `2026-04-23 18:00`).
3.  Click **ADD TO SCHEDULE**.
4.  **Leave the computer on**: TahaTyper will monitor the clock and start typing exactly at the scheduled time.
5.  **Pro Tip**: Make sure your Google Doc (or the target window) is open and focused before the scheduled time!

## 3. Human-Like Behavior 🤖
To ensure your typing looks authentic in Google Docs, TahaTyper Pro includes:
-   **Randomized Delays**: Mimics the natural rhythm of human fingers.
-   **Punctuation Pauses**: Automatically slows down after commas, periods, and line breaks.
-   **Simulated Mistakes**: Occasionally types the wrong character and immediately hits backspace to correct it—just like a real person!

## 4. Safety & Security 🔒
Your scheduled tasks are saved in `schedules.json` in your app folder. If you close the app, your schedules will still be there when you reopen it.

**Important**: For the scheduler to work, the TahaTyper app must be running in the background.

Enjoy the power of automated, scheduled typing with TahaTyper Pro! 🚀
